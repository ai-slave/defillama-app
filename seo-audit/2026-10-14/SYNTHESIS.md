## Windows

Two 28-day windows, shifted by one day, plus one single day:

- **Attached export** (PR1, and the prior-day / 7-day figures PR4 cites): 2026-09-14 through 2026-10-11.
- **Live pull** (PR2 and PR3): 2026-09-15 through 2026-10-12. Property `sc-domain:defillama.com`, `dataState: all`.
- **PR4's ranked day**: 2026-10-12 only. That day is inside the live window and outside the attached export.

`missed@3%` = `max(0, 0.03 × impressions − clicks)` everywhere. A query total and a page row for the same query are different slices: the page row is the URL we can edit, and the query total is the search. Summing them double-counts. Brand queries and bare protocol-name queries are not a 3% opportunity. Where a report sized them that way, the addressable number below uses a lower ceiling and says so.

Rows are ordered by the addressable figure. Row 1 stretches a 7-day template number to 28 days: the stretch is low confidence, and the missing server HTML is not. Rows 13 and 14 stay at the one-day figure because their query mix was never split.

## Commonalities

Findings reached independently by two or more reports. The ranked list below is the merged fix.

1. **`/dex-aggregators` answers neither "top" nor "what is".** PR2 and PR3. Three desktop queries ("top defi aggregators", "what are defi aggregators", "what are dex aggregators") sit at positions about 1.1–3.8 with 0 clicks. Both quote the same meta description, which lists 1inch, CowSwap, and OpenOcean and skips Jupiter. Missed clicks are 142 on the page rows and 143 on the query totals.
2. **The head query "tvl" is a definition SERP, and `/chains` is the URL that receives it.** PR1, PR2, and PR3. `/chains` has about 8,118 impressions, position ~5.7, 142 clicks, CTR 1.7%. The only DefiLlama URL on the live US first page is `/protocol/volta-finance`, whose FAQ asks "What is Volta Finance's TVL?". PR4 left `/chains` unranked because the page cleared 3% CTR on 12 Oct (183 clicks / 5,346 impressions). That is the page total, not this query.
3. **`/stablecoins` is titled as a chart and headed by the market-cap statistic.** PR1, PR2, PR3, and PR4, each from a different query. The title in `src/pages/stablecoins.tsx` is `Stablecoin Market Cap Chart, Supply & Peg Data - DefiLlama`. The visible H1 is "Total Stablecoins Market Cap". PR1 sizes the list family at 62 missed clicks. PR2 and PR3 size "usdt dominance" plus "usdt dominance chart" at 102. PR4 sizes the whole URL at 22.5 missed clicks on 12 Oct and notes a 202-character description and no JSON-LD. PR1 and PR2 agree the 28-day page-level missed@3% is 1,383, and PR2 says that total is mostly brand.
4. **USDT dominance is the same pair of queries in three reports.** PR1 records it so it is not mistaken for a CoinGecko gap. PR2 and PR3 both get missed@3% of 102 on the live window. The series on the page is each coin's share of stablecoin market cap (`getStablecoinDominance` in `src/containers/Stablecoins/utils.ts`). The live SERP for the chart query is TradingView's USDT.D.
5. **`/protocols/lending` throws away its own definition.** PR2 and PR3. "defi lending and borrowing protocols explained" is about 1,360 impressions, position ~6.9, 0 clicks, missed 41. The description string is `Protocols that allow users to borrow and lend assets` in `protocolCategoryConfig.Lending`. The meta description Google shows is the rankings sentence. PR3 names the 155-character gate; the code matches (see verification).
6. **"defi fee structures" hits `/fees` with 0 clicks.** PR2 and PR3. Page row: 889 impressions, position 4.1, missed 27. Query total: 1,126 impressions, position 5.21, missed 34, with `/fees/chains` at position 9.7. Title is `Protocol Fees Rankings - Fees Generated - DefiLlama`.
7. **Protocol titles are built in one place, and the meta description ignores the stored description.** PR1, PR3, and PR4 all land in `src/containers/ProtocolOverview/queries.tsx`. PR4 also notes live descriptions of 500–600 characters that open with a metric list. The stored `description` is a separate field, already rendered in the Protocol Information card.
8. **`/rwa` overview is a dashboard title plus a metric H1, on purpose.** PR1 and PR4. Both cite `src/containers/RWA/layout/pageCopy.ts`. Title `Real World Assets (RWA) Dashboard & Analytics - DefiLlama`. H1 `Total RWA Active AUM`.
9. **`/perps` already wins the volume query and loses the list phrasing and the definition.** PR1 and PR2. "perp dex volume" has hundreds of clicks and missed@3% of 0 in both. PR1's gap is "perp dex list" (8.3 missed). PR2's gap is that no answer engine cites the page.
10. **`/yields` is a rankings page whose rows are not in the server HTML, and it has no definition of yield farming.** PR4 (SSR) and PR2 (the query "yield farming", 400 impressions, position 11, 0 clicks, missed 12).
11. **Hubs do not pass `jsonLd`, and `src/components/SEO/index.tsx` emits it only when a page does.** PR2 and PR4. `src/layout/index.tsx` already forwards the prop.
12. **`robots.txt` is allow-all and `llms.txt` is not the citation surface.** PR2 and PR4. No answer in the 75-answer sample cited `llms.txt`.
